## Known differences from Figma / open items

1. **Expanded sidebar content.** Figma's `e` frames draw a different nav (Talent Search 142, Job Postings 142, …, a "Verified" stats card) than the app's verified talent sidebar. The app's `TalentSidebar` is used; only the active item (Messages) and layout follow Figma. Needs an owner decision.
2. **Expanded « toggle** is not in Figma (⚠️ ASSUMPTION — needed to collapse again).
3. **Unread frame (N-06)** keeps "All" highlighted and Unread "4" in the rail; the app highlights Unread and shows its live count (0 after mark-all-read).
4. **Figma action-frame heights are inconsistent** (28 / 31 / 42 / 47px around the same button), so row heights differ by up to ±9px; not copied.
5. Other filters/tabs (Jobs, Messages, …) are a plain type filter ⚠️ ASSUMPTION; no Figma frames for them. Quiet-hours / ping-setting controls are presentational.
6. Fonts: SF Pro Rounded is not installed on Windows, so text widths differ slightly from Figma renders there.
7. Not wired (no screens exist): Opportunities, Messages, Skills Lab, My Learning tabs. `/community` is the recruiter-chrome page, so Community was **not** routed from the talent nav.
8. **Phone appearance dial (2026-10-18, owner request).** On phones (< md) the Focused/Playful switch is a floating circle above the Ask Buddy button (`StyleDial.jsx`, rendered by `AskBuddyFab`): shows the current mode's icon, turns into ✕ when tapped and fans the two modes out (scale/translate with a soft overshoot, 45ms stagger; no motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`). Esc / outside tap closes. No Figma frame — ⚠️ ASSUMPTION. Its offset above the button must stay in sync with `AskBuddyFab`'s phone heights (54px, 42px below 700px tall).
